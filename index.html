  2. January 2026 – May 2026Pittsburgh, PA

    Graduate Research AssistantCarnegie Mellon University

    I wanted to know where inside a language model a financial comparison actually gets decided. I extracted activations across all 28 layers over more than five thousand mutual-fund comparison samples and trained linear probes on them. Input features were recoverable above 90% accuracy, with encoding peaking mid-stack, but the probe for the model's final decision trailed the probe for its inputs by 6.3 points — a measurable gap between what the model has represented and what it does with it.
