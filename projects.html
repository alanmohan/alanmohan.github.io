Sentiment steering against RAG

Published research First author IEEE ICC 2026

A retrieval-augmented system repeats what is in its corpus. So an attacker who can add a handful of documents can change what the model says about a person or a topic without ever touching the question being asked.

What I did

I built the black-box attack simulation and the detector that answers it. The attack poisons a slice of the corpus with sentiment-shifted passages about public officials; the defence inspects retrieved passages before they ever reach the generator, so it does not depend on which model is generating.

Approach

Passages are embedded with a MiniLM sentence encoder and retrieved through a FAISS index, then generated with Llama 3.1. Detection is two independent signals over the retrieved set: cosine similarity, which catches the near-duplicate phrasing that poisoning tends to introduce, and a z-score on the sentiment distribution, which catches passages whose polarity sits outside the corpus baseline. Either signal can flag a passage, which is what keeps recall high.

Why it matters

Retrieval is how most deployed language models get their facts, and a corpus is far easier to edit than a model. The detector works on retrieved passages rather than generations, so it transfers across generators and retrievers instead of being tuned to one stack.

Explainable intrusion detection for IoT

Published research Third of five authors Computers and Electrical Engineering

An intrusion detection system that flags traffic without saying why cannot be acted on. A network administrator needs to know which features drove the call before shutting down a device.

What I did

I built and evaluated the deep classifiers and the SHAP-driven feature selection step. The useful finding was not the accuracy — it was that the explanations were good enough to act on: the fifteen features SHAP ranked highest were sufficient on their own.

Approach

One-dimensional CNN, LSTM and Bi-LSTM classifiers, trained across four benchmark datasets so the result would not be an artefact of one capture: NSL-KDD, UNSW-NB15, TON-IoT and X-IIoTID. SMOTE handles the class imbalance that makes rare attacks look like noise. SHAP then produces global and local explanations, and retraining on only the top fifteen features tests whether those explanations are faithful.

Why it matters

A model that needs the full feature vector cannot run on the hardware IoT networks actually use. The fifteen-feature subset detected attacks including denial of service, ransomware and cross-site scripting above 92% accuracy on every one of the four datasets — evidence that the explanations were faithful, not decorative.

HOPE

Product Lead developer Multi-agent CBT platform

Cognitive behavioural therapy works and costs more than most people can pay. Most software answers to that are a single chatbot that forgets you between sessions.

What I did

I designed HOPE as an orchestrated team of agents rather than one prompt. A session coordinator decides which stage of a CBT session the conversation is in and hands off to specialists: a memory extractor that parses each message into thought patterns, therapy goals and session insights; a reply drafter grounded in a library of cognitive distortions and homework exercises; and a crisis detector that reads every inbound message for self-harm indicators before any reply is generated.

Approach

Long-term context lives in a vector store, with a rolling summary keeping sessions coherent without paying for the whole transcript on every turn. The crisis check sits in the message pipeline rather than in the model prompt, so it cannot be talked around.

This site

Build Designed and built it 2026

I needed somewhere to publish work that a technical reader could check rather than take on trust.

What I did

Three hand-written pages of semantic HTML, one stylesheet built on a custom-property design system, and a small amount of vanilla JavaScript for the theme toggle, the mobile menu, the project index you just came through, and the pipeline explainer above. Every diagram is inline SVG I drew, so it inherits the theme instead of shipping as a second set of images.

Approach

No framework, no build step, no dependency beyond two webfonts. The typographic rule underneath it: the serif makes claims, the monospace holds facts you can check. Everything JavaScript does is an enhancement — with it disabled the navigation is fully expanded, every project write-up is on the page, and the theme follows the operating system. Built with AI assistance, logged in full in the repository's prompt log.
