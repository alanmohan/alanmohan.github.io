Read the map carefully

These are police-recorded incidents, not a complete measure of personal risk. Locations are deliberately anonymised to nearby map points, reporting varies by place, and busy destinations can show more incidents simply because more people pass through them. Use this as one input—not as a label for a neighbourhood or a substitute for current local advice.

Crime data: UK Police open data. Maps: OpenStreetMap contributors. Geocoding: Nominatim.
